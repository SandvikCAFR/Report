Standing Off-Site Instructions:
While working on a customer's site;
1. Abide by customer site EHS rules and procedures.
2. Never start work on a site without a site induction.
3. Never work on any energised equipment (or equipment capable of being energised) without ISOLATING or verifying the isolation.
4. Never take any risks or short-cuts to do the job.
5. Only use the right tools for their intended purpose.
